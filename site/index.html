Previous Year Question Practice

What To Collect

  • University exam question papers by year and semester.
  • Model papers and internal exam papers.
  • Topic-wise grouped repeated questions.

How To Store Answers

  • Create your own solved answer notes for each paper.
  • Store expected key points rather than long copied text.
  • Add marks split: 2-mark, 8-mark, 13-mark, 15/16-mark.
This portal intentionally avoids reproducing copyrighted question papers or answer keys directly. Add only official/public-domain material or your own prepared solutions.
